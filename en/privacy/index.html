Scope

This policy covers the reviewed iOS app version 0.1.0 (build 1, TestFlight evaluation build) and its support website. The app includes Adventure across 10 worlds and 150 levels, Free Play, and Learn & Practice. This page will be updated if the applicable version or information handling changes.

Information stored on your device and its purposes

The app stores entered numbers, candidate notes, undo and redo history, the selected cell, input mode, hint-use count, unfinished games, puzzle, level and world clear records, journey completion records, settings and display language on your device. Older unfinished games archived during puzzle updates and migration records may also be retained.

These records support resuming play, showing progress, keeping settings, maintaining compatibility during puzzle updates, and validating and recovering saves. Save generations, ordering, timestamps and integrity hashes are also stored. Local processing of these records does not itself transmit them off your device.

SDKs, network use and registration

No app-specific data-transmission path, advertising, analytics or purchase SDK, WebView or account-registration path was identified in the reviewed product scripts and iOS settings. The app uses Godot and bundled libraries. Device language, screen and safe areas, input and time support display, interaction and saving. Apple’s information handling and the operator’s practices are described below.

Apple diagnostics and TestFlight

Apple handles diagnostic and usage information according to OS settings and the distribution method. TestFlight may provide developers with crash and usage information, tester details, and optional feedback. Apple’s own handling of information is distinguished from information the operator obtains and uses.

No app-specific analytics or diagnostic SDK will be added. The operator uses only the minimum necessary aggregate information provided by Apple. As a general rule, individual diagnostic logs are not exported or stored independently.

Retention and deletion of information managed by Apple follow Apple’s systems and policies. The operator does not guarantee Apple’s storage location or a uniform deletion deadline. Information users voluntarily send in support requests is handled under the support retention and deletion policy below.

Retention, backups and deletion

Progress and settings remain on your device so you can resume play. Saves use multiple generations, delta records, temporary files, legacy archives and quarantined damaged files. Normal generations are updated; no time-based expiry process for archives or quarantined files is provided. Integrity checking is not encryption.

There is no in-app operation to erase all game data. Apple’s general instructions allow deletion through Settings → General → iPhone Storage → the app → Delete App. Offload App keeps documents and data. Deletion can cause loss of progress; do not delete the app if you wish to keep your records.

No app-specific cloud or account sync is provided. Copies in OS backups are managed separately from deletion on the device, and restoration and retention depend on Apple settings and services. Restoration is not guaranteed. Support cannot remotely erase saves on your device.

Support information, retention and deletion

Contact: piyosta25@gmail.com. If you send a support email, we receive your sender email address, message and optional attachments to answer your request, investigate issues and follow up as needed. Do not send unnecessary personal information, passwords or verification codes. Email is handled through Gmail; this does not mean that a Google SDK is included in the game.

Within 180 days after a support case is closed, we delete its contents from the operator-managed mailbox, including Trash, and manually saved copies. Where a legal retention duty or a specific dispute requires retention, only the relevant information is kept for the necessary purpose and period, and is deleted when no longer needed.

To request deletion, normally write from the same email address used for your support request, with the subject “Personal data deletion request”. We check only what is necessary to match your request and normally do not ask for identity documents. We aim to delete the information or provide a status update within 30 days. If further verification or an extension is needed, we explain the reason and expected timing.

Deletion timing for provider-managed backups and logs, including Google’s, follows the provider’s policies and is not subject to a uniform deadline guaranteed by the operator. Game records on your device cannot be remotely deleted through email.

Website access information

This website has no added analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts, embedded content, submission forms, or code that writes cookies or browser storage. Language selection uses ordinary links. GitHub Pages serves the site, and GitHub logs and stores IP addresses for security. Hosting retention and deletion follow GitHub’s policies; no specific retention period or storage location is guaranteed.

Third-party services

The game has no feature that sends progress records to advertising or analytics providers. Apple distribution and diagnostics, Google/Gmail for support, and GitHub Pages for website delivery handle information for their respective purposes. Use of information obtained from Apple is described in the diagnostics section.

Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be shown on this page with the effective or update date and applicable version. For questions about information handling, contact piyosta25@gmail.com.
