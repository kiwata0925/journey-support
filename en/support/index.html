ADVENTURE

Continue the journey

Adventure has one puzzle per level. Clear levels in order and reach the Sudoku challenge guarded by each world's ancient guardian. Adventure levels do not have a difficulty selector.

LEARN & PRACTICE

Practice the basics

Open Learn & Practice from Home to explore rows, columns, 3×3 boxes, single numbers, finding the only place, and notes. Practice does not change Adventure or Free Play progress.

FREE PLAY

Choose your challenge

Free Play offers Easy, Medium and Hard. Its progress is separate from Adventure, and unfinished puzzles can be saved and resumed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter numbers and notes?

Select a cell, then tap a number. You can also use number-first input. Turn on Notes to enter candidates manually, and use Undo or Redo to revise entries. Hints include an explanation.

Can I play without an internet connection?

The included puzzles can be played offline. Internet access is needed to view the website or send support email.

Are unfinished puzzles and progress saved?

Puzzle entries, notes, input history, unfinished Adventure and Free Play sessions, completion records and settings are stored on your device. Multiple save generations help with recovery.

If the app reports a save failure, check the device's available storage and use Retry Save. Deleting the app before investigating a problem may cause local progress to be lost.

Can I transfer progress to a new phone or reinstall?

The app has no account or app-specific cloud sync. OS backups and restoration depend on Apple settings and services, so transfer cannot be guaranteed in all situations. Do not delete the app if you want to keep your progress.

Can I replay a completed level?

Selecting a completed level offers View Completed Grid, Replay, and Back. Replaying keeps your existing completion record.

What if I get stuck?

Open Learn & Practice from Home to practice the basics. During play, Hint explains a step, and Notes lets you record candidates manually. Undo and Redo are also available.

By default, the app highlights duplicates in rows, columns and 3×3 boxes. To also flag entries that differ from the completed solution, change the solution-check setting.

Do guardian levels have combat or a time limit?

Guardian levels use ordinary 9×9 Sudoku. There is no HP, combat, time limit or special interference. Notes, Hint and Undo remain available.

Can I switch between Japanese and English?

In Settings, choose the device-language option, Japanese or English. The preference is saved. Automatic selection uses English when the device language is not Japanese.

Are there advertisements or in-app purchases?

This version has no advertising or in-app purchase feature.
